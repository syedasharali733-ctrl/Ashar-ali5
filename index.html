BMW X5 Review

The BMW X5 is a luxury SUV that blends performance, comfort, and technology. With a 300HP engine, advanced safety features, and premium interiors, it is designed for drivers who demand excellence. Compared to rivals like Mercedes GLE and Audi Q7, the X5 offers a balanced mix of sporty handling and family practicality.
